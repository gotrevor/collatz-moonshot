## Ranked candidate threads, by next discriminating move

### 1. Finite trajectory packing beyond the Christoffel class — best bounded research test, low headline payoff

- **Exact result:** paper theorem above, including finite residence `L+1=O(X^beta log X)` for nonrepeating integer traces with max height X and beta=log_32(243/8)<1 (`finite-packing-christoffel.md:25-75`).  Formal `OrbitPacking.block_card_le` currently assumes all-depth `IterateSeparated`, though its proof uses injection only at the block depth (`FrontA/OrbitPacking.lean:182-226`).
- **Missing statement:** a concrete *covered* family of first-crossing words whose admitted integer traces have `max_{t<=m} T^t(n)=O(n+K)` and `K` distinct odd values.  Without the height bound, the finite packing conclusion does not generalize; without handling repeats, it cannot exclude cycles.
- **Mechanism/test:** first refactor the block lemma on paper to single-depth injectivity; for each candidate word family derive an exact coefficient/numerator bound on **every prefix**, then test it against the known P6 family and the Christoffel control.  If any family includes too many words but fails height, stop before a Lean lap.  A cheap numerical screen is `max_t T^t(n)/(n+K)` on exact-admission examples, paired with an exact symbolic bound rather than a sampled ratio.
- **Unlock:** excludes nonrepeating realizations in a provably broad low-height class, perhaps yielding a finite-or-cycle reduction for that class.  Feasibility of the *restricted formal theorem* high (~80%); of a meaningful coverage theorem low (~10%); of Collatz itself negligible.  No literature priority claim.

### 2. Pointwise full-residue exclusion at first crossing — central mathematical crux, presently no mechanism

- **Exact result:** `At`, `three_mul_numer_le` (`FrontA/FirstCrossing.lean:14-69`), run-product bound (`FirstCrossingRuns.lean:116-176`), few-run theorem (`LogTwoThreeDigits.lean:290`), and exact overshoot congruence (`FirstCrossingResidue.lean:59`).  `N_epsilon` is not CST; its complement `C_epsilon` must exclude all many-run survivors (`mechanism-search.md:36-99`).
- **Missing statement:** for every first-crossing word, the canonical residue `c(v)>=2` has `D c(v)>N(v)`, equivalently CST, or an independently specified unbounded subclass plus a coverage rule.  A lower bound on `[N*3^{-K}]_D` must beat the O(K) overshoot window *pointwise*; entropy/discrepancy expectation does not.
- **Mechanism/test:** require a proposed order-sensitive, admission-preserving inequality over jointly realized states.  Test first on the Christoffel false candidates, the P6 rational families, 2305/2313, and 5n+1 (where it must fail); measure the exact integer exclusion margin, not average residue distribution.  `experiments/parity_reconstruction.py near-cycle` already supplies the instrument.  An unbounded-class lemma with a nontrivial coverage/decomposition is the research gate (`collatz-headline-blueprint-2026-09-18.md`, DIRECTION:8-63).
- **Unlock:** CST would exclude nontrivial cycles, but `CrossingExists`/nondivergence remains separate.  Potential magnitude very high; present feasibility very low (<5% for a bounded attempt) because no surviving inequality is on the board.

### 4. Primitive cycle compression / new difference identity — high payoff, no current candidate

- **Exact result:** power degeneracy of naive compression and denominator bounds has been proved; primitive rewiring exists (`FrontB/Powers.lean` and `FRONT-B-ROUTES.md:1-75`). - **Missing statement:** a transformation of every primitive nontrivial *integral* cycle to one with <=91 circuits, or a new rotation-difference identity whose odd denominator factor is uniformly bounded.  An unspecified constant does not close the cycle front (DIRECTION:835-868).
- **Mechanism/test:** any proposed transformation must preserve integrality and nontriviality; run it symbolically on the negative -17 sign control, primitive rational short-run families, and all rotations of small words.  A word-only simplification that loses integrality fails immediately.  Test new identity classes against `experiments/knight_reach.py`'s sporadic-hit census before formalization.
- **Unlock:** Front B, subject to explicit bound and finite survivors.  Feasibility very low (<5%) for the next bounded attempt; this is a destination, not a ready work package.

### 5. Floor-preserving Tao saturation — separate divergence route, weak current leverage

- **Exact result:** divergent orbits have arbitrarily high eventual floors (`Rigidity/Drift.lean`); `mem_taoGood_of_reachesValue` shows that growing a backward basin around one fixed seed remains compatible with Tao 2019 (`FRONT-A-ROUTES.md:55-89,120-169`).  Barrier-tree probes show high-floor harmonic signal for sampled unit seeds but large nonuniformity (`FRONT-A-ROUTES.md:170-210`).  The Sept 22 reciprocal-summability result constrains forward divergent *single* orbits, not moving backward basin overlap.
- **Missing statement:** from high divergent tail seeds, obtain enough floor-preserving backward harmonic mass with controlled overlap to contradict Tao's logarithmic almost-all theorem.  A single-seed growth estimate alone does not.
- **Mechanism/test:** formulate the overlap/floor-preservation inequality for two moving seeds before any new table.  Probe the worst existing `d=1000052` sample and 3-divisible spine control; if the required uniform coefficient decays with seed, reject that precise inequality.  Never infer a theorem from finite census.
- **Unlock:** NoDivergentOrbit, leaving cycle exclusion separate.  Feasibility very low; no Sept 18–25 new mechanism clears the repo gate.
