# Dubickas - "Powers of rational numbers modulo 1 lying in short intervals"

- **Venue**: Results Math. 57 (2010) 23-31, doi:10.1007/s00025-009-0001-0.  Cornell/Springer, read 2026-10-05.
- **Thm 1.1** (`p = 2q - 1`): some `ξ > 0` has `‖ξ(2 - 1/q)^n - t/(q - 1)‖ < (q - 1)/(2q - 1)`.  For `q = 2` this only recovers AFS's `‖ξ(3/2)^n‖ < 1/3`.
- **Thm 1.3** (`p ≥ 2q + 1`): centred-arc bounds.  Not applicable to 3/2.
- **Verdict for arc-trap**: no new 3/2 constant in either direction.
