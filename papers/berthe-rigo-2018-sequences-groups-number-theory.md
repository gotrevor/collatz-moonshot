# Berthé & Rigo (eds.) - *Sequences, Groups, and Number Theory*

- **Venue**: Birkhäuser, Trends in Mathematics, 2018, ISBN 978-3-319-69152-7.  Trevor's download, 2026-10-05.
- Contains Becher-Carton, "Normal numbers and computer science", plus chapters on Mahler functions, numeration and automata.
- **For arc-trap**: no `(3/2)^n` constants.  It mentions Mahler's Z-numbers only in passing.  It is more relevant to the normal-numbers repo.
