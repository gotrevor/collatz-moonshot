## 🧠 Context to carry forward

**The mathematical content, in one line.** On the residue subclass `h = 2 + 95t`,
`lowerC h` factors as exactly `5 * x` with `x = 3349 + 124032t`, and the five-head
quadratic relation `Q5` holds for `(n, c, d) = (x, b, z)` where `b = (15x+1)/76`,
`z = (15x+5)/64`. So the pair of *strictly smaller* labels `[x, z]` legally
introduces the head `lowerC h` together with the companion `b`.

**The one real design decision.** The kickoff offered two routes — reverse the
certificate equality, or copy `five_head_pair_of_Q5`'s `certificateValue` proof
structure. I took neither literally, and took a third that is strictly better:
I added a *reusable* lemma `five_head_pair_of_Q5_rev` with hypotheses **identical**
to `five_head_pair_of_Q5`, concluding the exchange in the opposite direction
`legalPairMove [n,d] ([n,d],[5n,c])`. It invokes the forward lemma, destructures
its `legalPairMove` conjunction, and reuses the `certificateValue` equality via
`.symm`, re-permuting only the positive-odd side condition. Consequence: there is
exactly **one** rational-arithmetic (`field_simp`/`nlinarith`) proof in the file
tree rather than two, and the `observed*`/`lower*` families in `FiveHead.lean` now
get the reverse orientation for free. Copying the `certificateValue` structure
would have worked but duplicated the only genuinely delicate proof in the area.

**Everything else was elementary, exactly as the kickoff predicted.** No new
assumptions were introduced. `lowerC (2+95t) = 5 * recursiveX t` is `ring`. The
`Q5` obligation `5b(3x+1)(3z+1) = z(15x+1)(3b+1)` is a degree-3 polynomial
identity in `t`, closed by `ring`; I sanity-checked it by hand at `t = 0` before
writing any Lean (both sides `78239555840`), which is why it went green first try.
The height and parity/3-freeness conditions are all `omega`: each linear form has
a `t`-coefficient that is even and divisible by 3, with constant residues
`3349,785,661,16745 ≡ 1,2,1,2 mod 3` and all odd.

**What this result is NOT** — carry this framing forward, it is the honest reading
and the kickoff insisted on it. It is one symbolic supply *step* on a residue
subclass. It does not assert that `x, z` are themselves in the subclass, that they
are borrowable, or that any recursion terminates. It is not recursive closure, not
a repair theorem, and **no general Collatz claim**.
