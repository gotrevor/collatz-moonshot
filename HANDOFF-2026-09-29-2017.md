- The whole lap hinged on one mechanical question: how to evaluate `tstep^[6] x` on a
  *symbolic* `x = c + d*t`. The answer that worked: `Function.iterate` on the numeral
  `6` is **definitionally** the six-fold composition, so a private helper
  `tstep_chain6` just does `show tstep (tstep (… a)) = g` and `rw`s six one-step
  hypotheses. No `Function.iterate_succ_apply` rewriting, no numeral-vs-`n+1`
  unification fight. This generalizes to any fixed-length orbit lemma in this repo.
- Each one-step identity is uniformly `simp only [tstep]; split <;> omega`. `split`
  exposes the parity hypothesis (`(c + d*t) % 2 = 0` or its negation) and `omega`
  then handles parity, the truncated `/2`, and the affine arithmetic in one goal.
  Key discipline (kickoff was right): pin every intermediate expression; never let a
  composed orbit expression reach a single `omega`.
- `refillT_formula` (`9 * refillT k + 1 = 64 ^ k`) is induction + `pow_succ` + `omega`,
  which works because `omega` treats `64 ^ k` as an atom and the relation is linear in
  that atom plus `refillT k`.
