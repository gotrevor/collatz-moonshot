## 🧠 Context to carry forward

The crux was always the packing count — everything downstream is conditional on
it — so it went first.  Two departures from the source note made it tractable,
and they are the reusable ideas:

1. **Five-step dyadic shells.**  `[2^(5t), 2^(5t+5))` is *itself* the single
   aligned block `q = 0, m = 5t+5`, and `3m/5 = 3t+3` is exact.  So the paper's
   interval-to-block covering reduction (its eq. (4)) is never needed, and the
   resulting series is geometric in `t` with rational ratios `27/32`, `243/256`.
2. **Integer Chernoff weight.**  The paper weights bad words by `(3/2)^r`; I use
   `2^r`, since `(1+2) < 2·2^(3/5)` still holds.  The entire combinatorial
   argument then lives in `ℕ` — no `rpow`, no fractional exponents anywhere.
   `weightSum_le : ∑_{s<2^m} 2^(ones (traceWord s m)) ≤ 3^m` is proved from a
   one-step injection `t ↦ (3t+2) mod 2^k`, NOT from the exact binomial count
   (which would have needed the full word↔residue bijection).

**Defect found in the source note, repaired not patched:** it asserts `b_k → ∞`
from divergence, but this repo's `Diverges` is *unboundedness*
(`∀ M, ∃ k, M ≤ step^[k] n`), not `Tendsto atTop`.  The fix is the pre-existing
`Rigidity/Drift.exists_floor_of_diverges` lifted to `tstep` via
`exists_step_count` (`exists_floor_tstep`).  Nothing in the chain assumes
tendsto.

**Design constraint honoured throughout:** no `StoppingCorrect`, no
`NoNontrivialCycle`, no verification range.  Positive nontrivial cycles are
allowed by *both* sides — the reverse implication manufactures the crossing
*from* the cycle (`numer v > 0` because a nonempty all-even cycle would strictly
decrease), which is exactly why it doesn't exclude them.

Only `∑ 1/y_j` bounded is ever needed — never the paper's uniform tail `B(F)`,
which served its §4/§7 remainder discussion and is out of scope.
